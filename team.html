The people behind Turiya Trails

Grounded companions, not entertainers

People trust people, not brochures. Here is who you will actually be walking, watching birds and sitting under the stars with.

No stock photographs here. Portraits of the team will appear once we have taken them properly.

N

Nishant

Lead guide and host

Runs Oakakhi Stays in Ranikhet and leads every trek in person. BMC mountaineering certified. The person you will meet first, chai in hand, before anything else is discussed.

  • Himalayan safety
  • Hospitality
  • Forest ecology
“I've hosted people at Oakakhi for years. What I want now is to actually walk them into the forest, not just point at it from the porch.”
S

Sachin

Astro guide and storyteller

Training with a stargazing company near Ramnagar and bringing every technique back to Turiya's astro nights. Believes the best fact is the one wrapped inside a story.

  • Constellation navigation
  • Astrophotography
  • Campfire storytelling
“Most people have never really looked up. My job on an astro night isn't to recite facts, it's to get out of the way so the sky can do that.”
K

Naveen (Kannu)

Field documentarian

Born in these hills. Shoots every walk, every dawn, every quiet moment that would otherwise go unrecorded. Also first on the trail when the group needs steady hands.

  • Camera craft
  • Birding
  • First aid
“I grew up in these hills. Filming them for Turiya Trails is the first time work has meant showing people my own backyard properly.”
What connects them

From these hills, for these hills

  • All three live in or near the Kumaun hills, not flown in for the season
  • Trained in Himalayan safety and basic first aid
  • Speak when the mountain speaks, silent when silence is needed
  • None of them perform for a camera, including Naveen's own
