Our base

Every journey begins at Oakakhi

A small homestay in the oak forest above Ranikhet, at around 1,800 metres. Chai first. Nothing is discussed until chai is in hand.

Oakakhi Stays, Ranikhet

A home, not a base camp

Oakakhi is run by Nishant, our lead guide, who has hosted people here for years. It is where you arrive, where you eat, and where you come back to after a day on the trail.

The Kalika forest trail starts close by, and so do most of our forest walks and birding mornings. The sky above it is dark enough for our astro nights to happen right here, on the rooftop or in the meadow nearby.

We do not sell village life as a separate ticket. It is simply how the days already work: chai with Nishant's family, home-cooked Kumaoni meals, and mornings that start slowly because the mountain is not going anywhere.

Getting here

The road to Ranikhet

Ranikhet is in Almora district, Uttarakhand. Times below are approximate and depend on the roads and the season.

By train

Kathgodam

The nearest railway station, about 80 km away. Roughly three hours by road through the hills.

By air

Pantnagar

The nearest airport, roughly four hours by road.

By road

From Delhi

About 350 km, a long day's drive via Moradabad and Haldwani, or an overnight train to Kathgodam.

Tell us how you are travelling and we will help you plan the last stretch. Find Oakakhi on Google Maps

Good to know

Before you arrive

  • Mobile signal is patchy at Oakakhi, by design. Ranikhet town has signal.
  • Days are pleasant most of the year. Nights are cool, and from October to February you will want warm layers.
  • Meals are home-cooked and Kumaoni. Tell us about dietary needs in advance.
  • Bring sturdy shoes, a headlamp and less than you think.
