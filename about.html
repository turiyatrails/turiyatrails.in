About Turiya Trails

Not another adventure company

A guide, an astro storyteller, a documentarian and a small homestay in Ranikhet, building something plainer.

Why we started

Too loud, too fast, or trying to sell an adventure

Every trek we found nearby was either too loud, too fast, or trying to sell us an adventure. Kalika forest, Oakakhi, the Kumaun skies, none of it needed dressing up. It needed someone to slow down enough to actually see it.

So a guide, an astro storyteller, a documentarian and a small homestay in Ranikhet built something plainer: walk slowly, watch closely, ask real questions, and let the mountains do the rest.

The name comes from Turiya, a state of awareness beneath waking, dreaming and deep sleep. Always present, rarely noticed. That is what these journeys are quietly built around.

What Turiya means

“When do you feel most like yourself?”

A walking question
Right people, not more people

The Turiya oath

Words we walk by

I will walk with awareness, not just legs.

I will be kind to animals and to the Earth.

I will leave nature better than I found it.

I will stay curious. Ask the harder questions.

I will respect silence and fellow travellers.

I will return not just changed, but clear.

The people

Grounded companions, not entertainers

Trained in Himalayan terrain, safety and first aid, birdwatching and astro storytelling. They speak when the mountain speaks, and stay silent when silence is needed.
