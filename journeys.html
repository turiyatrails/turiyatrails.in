Journeys

Journeys that ask nothing of you except attention

Small groups of 4 to 10. Slow days in the Kumaun Himalaya, based at Oakakhi in Ranikhet. Every journey includes our forest walks, shared meals, and evenings that end under the sky.

Prices are per person and include stays, meals, guiding and transport during the journey. Dates run in small batches and are shared on WhatsApp. Want something in between? Any day experience can be added to a journey, or we can shape one around your group.
