Walk to notice, not to conquer

Three trails, graded honestly

These are the treks we know and lead ourselves. Each one also lives inside a longer journey, but you can walk any of them on its own. Groups of 4 to 10, led by our BMC certified guide.

Dates are released in small batches, not year round. Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is opening next.

Beginner

Pandavkholi

Kukuchina, near Dwarahat

A gentle forest climb to a high meadow tied to the years the Pandavas spent in hiding. The trail passes near the Mahavatar Babaji cave, and the meadow at the top is the kind of place people stop talking without being asked.

Trail

A steady forest path from Kukuchina village. Comfortable for first time trekkers who can walk a few hours.

Intermediate

Pinnath

Near Kausani

A climb through oak and rhododendron to a temple ridge at 2,750 metres. The clouds usually arrive before you do, and on a clear day the high Himalaya stands in a line across the north.

Ask about the next batch

Trail

5 km each way

A sustained climb. You should be comfortable walking uphill for a few hours at altitude.

Altitude

2,750 metres

High enough to feel it. We walk slowly on purpose.

Challenging

Bharatkot

Almora district

The highest peak in its range, and the hardest walking we offer. The trek is usually taken over two days with a night in camp on the mountain. It asks for fitness and gives back a sky you will not forget.
