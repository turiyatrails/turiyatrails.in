Silent forest walks

Walking as a practice of presence

In oak and rhododendron forests that have stood for centuries, we walk slowly enough to notice what rushing past would erase.

The experience

Walking as a practice, not a sport

We live in a world that has turned walking into exercise and forests into photo opportunities. Our silent forest walks are something different, a deliberate return to presence, where the goal is not a destination but attention.

The oak and rhododendron forests around Ranikhet are among the most intact in the Kumaun Himalaya. Walking through them slowly, you begin to notice the texture of bark, the sound of wind through different trees, and the way light changes through the canopy at different times of day.

“The forest does not care how fast you walk. It rewards only those who stop.”

The Turiya Trails way
What we notice

The forest up close

Banj oak

The defining tree of the Kumaun forests. Wide-trunked, covered in moss and ferns, and long valued by local communities as a keeper of water on the hillside.

Buransh

The rhododendron is Uttarakhand's state tree. It blooms red in March and April, and locals make a deep red juice from the flowers.

Moss and ferns

The floor of a healthy Kumaun forest is carpeted in them. Our guides can name many and explain the part they play.

Forest light

Early morning brings shafts through mist, afternoon brings dappled patches. Cameras struggle with it. You have to be there.

How it works

What a session looks like

  • Starts at sunrise or late afternoon, never midday
  • Complete silence for the first 30 minutes
  • The guide leads, stops often, and points without speaking
  • A walking question at the start, to carry with you
  • Slower than feels natural at first
  • Chai and conversation after, back at Oakakhi
The trail

The Kalika forest trail, starting from Oakakhi. Gentle gradients on well-kept paths. Suitable for all fitness levels.

Duration

Two to three hours. Some walks cover three kilometres in three hours. That is not inefficiency, that is the point.

Group size

A maximum of six people. Silence is harder to hold in larger groups.
