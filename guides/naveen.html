Naveen (Kannu)

Born in these hills. Never far from a camera.

Naveen, Kannu to everyone who knows him, grew up in these hills. He leads treks alongside Nishant, and is usually the first on the trail when the group needs steady hands.

His stillness has a camera in it. He is the one who stops at the edge of a meadow and waits for the light, or wanders off at dawn and comes back with a photograph nobody else saw.

Walk with Naveen and you will see the hills the way he does: slowly, and from the inside.
