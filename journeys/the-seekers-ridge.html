The Seeker's Ridge

For those who want the silence to cost something. A long, honest walk to Bharatkot with one night in camp on the mountain.

Duration
3 nights, 4 days
Route
Ranikhet and Bharatkot
Level
Challenging
Price
₹19,500 pp

For those who want the silence to cost something. Bharatkot is the highest peak in its range near Almora, and the walk to it is long and honest.

One night is spent in camp on the mountain. The night in camp includes the sky, the fire, and very little else. That is the point.

Day by day

  1. Day one

    Arrive at Oakakhi. The sunset forest walk, dinner, and an early night. The briefing happens at the fire.

  2. Days two and three

    The Bharatkot trek, taken over two days with a camp night on the mountain. Led by our BMC certified lead guide.

  3. Day four

    Descend, return to Oakakhi for a proper meal, and leave when you are ready.

From the trail

Photographs from our own walks on this route.

The trek

Bharatkot · Challenging · Almora district. The highest peak in its range, and the hardest walking near Ranikhet. Usually taken over two days with a night in camp on the mountain. It asks for fitness and gives back a sky you will not forget.

More about Bharatkot

Who it suits

  • Fit, experienced walkers
  • People who want a real physical effort
  • Anyone drawn to a night on a mountain

How fit you need to be

Long distances and real climbs over two days. We talk honestly with you about readiness before confirming.

Included

  • Three nights, including one camp night
  • All meals
  • Camping equipment
  • The full trek with a certified guide
  • Local transport

Not included

  • Travel to Ranikhet and back
  • Personal expenses
  • Travel insurance

When to go

For fit walkers. Batches run outside monsoon and deep winter. Dates are shared on WhatsApp.

Before you book

Where do journeys start?

Almost every journey starts at Oakakhi Stays, our home in Ranikhet. The nearest railway station is Kathgodam, about three hours away by road. Tell us how you are travelling and we will help you plan the last stretch.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp or use the enquiry form. We talk first about what you are looking for, then share dates, the full plan and how to confirm your place. No booking is final until we have spoken.

I am travelling from abroad. Can I join?

Yes, and you are very welcome. Our page for international travellers covers getting to Ranikhet from Delhi, visas, permits, seasons, money and what to pack. Prices are in rupees, with approximate US dollar prices alongside.

What happens in an emergency?

Our guides carry first aid supplies and are trained in immediate response. In a real emergency, getting you to help fast is the only priority.
